02 / THE PHILOSOPHICAL QUESTION

How free is a choice
when the options
are selected for us?

JOHN STUART MILL · INDIVIDUALITY

Choosing for yourself

In On Liberty, Mill connects individuality with developing judgment rather than simply following custom. Applied to feeds, this invites us to ask whether recommendations support our interests or make alternatives harder to notice.

On Liberty, Chapter III ↗

JEAN BAUDRILLARD · SIMULATION

Taking the feed for the world

Baudrillard examines how models and signs can shape what people experience as reality. We use this as a lens for asking whether repeated images of success or beauty become our standards of normal life.

Simulacra and Simulation ↗

These are applications of philosophical ideas, not claims that either thinker studied today’s platforms. A personalized feed alone does not prove simulation or the loss of freedom.
